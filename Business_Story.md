# Business Story

## Business Problem
The e-commerce business must identify which product categories and cities are delivering the strongest revenue performance and determine whether customer segments such as gender influence order value.

## Objective
- Use the cleaned sales dataset to reveal the top-performing categories and regions.
- Validate the existing analysis with statistical evidence.
- Create a narrative that supports strategic decisions and growth planning.

## Analysis
The dataset contains 1000 cleaned order records, with 12 columns covering order details, customer demographics, product category, revenue, and quantity.

Key analysis steps:
- Reviewed category and city-level sales performance.
- Analyzed customer demographics by gender and age.
- Calculated total and average sales across categories and cities.
- Performed statistical testing to evaluate gender-based differences in order value.

## Key Findings
- `Electronics` is the top revenue category by total sales.
- `Grocery` has the highest average revenue per order, indicating strong per-transaction value.
- `Patna`, `Kolkata`, and `Bengaluru` are the leading cities for total revenue.
- `Bengaluru` has the strongest average order value among cities.
- Gender does not significantly affect average order total, so marketing should emphasize category and city segmenting.
- `Unit_Price` and `Quantity` are the primary numeric drivers of total sales.

## Business Impact
- The business can prioritize product investment in Electronics and Grocery to maximize revenue.
- Regional marketing programs for Patna, Kolkata, and Bengaluru will capture high-value demand.
- Categories with lower average order values, such as Fashion and Furniture, should be reviewed for pricing or bundling improvement.
- A gender-neutral revenue strategy is appropriate for average order value, freeing resources for more impactful segmentation.

## Recommendations
- Invest in promotional campaigns and inventory availability for Electronics and Grocery.
- Expand targeted offers for high-value cities while optimizing underperforming city performance.
- Use pricing strategies to increase the average order value in Fashion and Furniture.
- Monitor category and regional performance regularly using the Power BI dashboard.
